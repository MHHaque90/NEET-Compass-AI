# Dental Regulator — National Dental Commission (NDC)

> Sprint 3.0. The current authoritative dental regulator for BDS.

## Current authority

The **National Dental Commission (NDC)**, established under the *National
Dental Commission Act, 2023*, is the apex regulatory body for dental education
and the dental profession in India.

The **Dental Council of India (DCI)** is the **historical** regulator. Older
datasets may use the DCI name, but DCI is **not** the current authority. Use
NDC as the authority going forward; treat DCI-named data as legacy.
