# Source Conflict Strategy

> Sprint 3.0. Field-ownership matrix and conflict resolution. Only ownership
> relationships supported by research are listed; no authority relationships
> are invented.

## Principles

1. The **field owner** is the authority whose publication is definitive for
   that field.
2. When the field owner is unavailable or corrupt, fall back to the secondary
   authority and flag provenance — never silently blend.
3. **Recognition/intake** and **counselling seats** are distinct concepts owned
   by different authorities and must never be merged without provenance.
4. Institutional self-reported data is always subordinate to the regulator.

## Conflict resolution rules

- **Recognition vs seats:** if a college's NMC status and an MCC/state seat
  matrix disagree, they are answering different questions — keep both, with
  provenance, rather than "resolving".
- **NMC intake vs counselling seats:** NMC approved intake is the primary
  authority for sanctioned seats; MCC/state seat matrices are primary for the
  seats actually offered in a round. Never treat them as automatically
  equivalent.
- **Regulator vs college:** the regulator (NMC / NDC) wins over college
  self-reported data for recognition, intake, status.
- **MCC vs state:** for a state-quota seat, the state authority wins; for an
  AIQ/central seat, MCC wins. The scope of the record determines the owner.
- **Historical regulator naming:** for BDS data, NDC is the current authority
  even where older datasets carry the DCI name.

## Non-listed relationships

Relationships not listed above (e.g. NTA as authority for seats, college as
authority for recognition) are **not** claimed and must not be inferred.
