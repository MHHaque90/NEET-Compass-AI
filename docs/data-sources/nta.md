## What was verified (access date 2026-08-12)

- `nta.ac.in` is the official National Testing Agency portal.
- `neet.nta.nic.in` is the official NEET UG portal.
- Specific bulletin and result **dataset URLs** were not individually verified
  this pass; the domain purpose (NEET UG examination portal) is confirmed.

## Dataset families (not individually verified at URL level)

- Examination information bulletin
- Result (scorecard / rank card)
- Notices / schedule
- Official statistical information (result statistics) where available

## Course applicability

MBBS and BDS (NEET UG is the common entrance for MBBS/BDS admissions).

## Counselling scope

NATIONAL examination; it feeds both MCC (all-India quota) and state counselling.

## Privacy

Candidate PII is **out of scope**. Only aggregate/official statistics and
published notices are documented here. No personal candidate data is collected
or documented.

## Historical availability

See historical-availability.md. The 2026 portal is live; earlier-year dataset
availability is NOT VERIFIED.

## Notes / limitations

- NTA provides the examination, not the counselling allocation. For seats and
  allotments the authorities are MCC and the respective state counselling
  bodies (see source-conflicts.md).
